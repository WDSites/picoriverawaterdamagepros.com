Water Damage Help in a City With a Long Memory

Montebello covers 8.37 square miles about 8 miles east of downtown Los Angeles. Its past explains some of what homeowners ask us today, so this page follows that history and ties each chapter to something you can do. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock whenever a loss cannot wait.

Anyone dispatched to a Montebello address is a licensed and insured restoration professional. They bring moisture meters, thermal imaging cameras and LGR dehumidifiers.

From Newmark to Montebello, 1920 Onward

The city was formerly called Newmark and incorporated on October 16, 1920. The Newmark townsite sits near the railroad tracks around Los Angeles Avenue, 1st Street, Cleveland Avenue and 5th Street. If your building is in that area, ask the owner or neighbors about past repairs. Earlier patch jobs can hide damage, and a moisture map shows what is really wet.

Oil Wells, the Montebello Hills and What Lies Below

Standard Oil discovered oil in the Montebello Hills in 1917, and oil fields replaced agriculture. The old city motto called Montebello the City of Flowers With an Oil Well Payroll. History cannot tell you why your wall is wet. If you live on hillside ground, note which way water runs toward the house, and tell the crew so they trace surface and interior sources.

A Mission Moved by a Rio Hondo Flood

Mission Vieja was founded in 1771 near San Gabriel Boulevard and the Rio Hondo, and a flood moved the mission about six years later. The lesson is old but practical. Homeowner policies generally do not cover flood, so check the FEMA Flood Map Service Center for your address and ask your insurer about separate flood coverage.

Renters Hold More Than Half of Montebello Homes

Of Montebello's 20,308 housing units, 55.6 percent are renter-occupied. Water often comes from a neighbor's unit or a shared line. If you rent, write to your landlord the day you see the leak and keep photos. Owners of rentals should call for mitigation fast, because wet drywall and carpet worsen while approvals wait.

When water is spreading in Montebello

Active flow comes first. Stop the source if it is safe, then call our 24/7 dispatch line. Stay away from water near outlets, panels or gas appliances and call the utility or 911 before you call us.

Before We Arrive: If Water Is Still Spreading in Montebello

Find and close the valve

Look under the fixture for a small shutoff. Failing that, turn the house main until the flow stops.

Be careful with breakers

Flip off circuits for wet rooms only when you can reach a dry panel. If the floor is wet around it, leave it.

Clear the floor

Lift electronics, paperwork and cushions to dry surfaces. Slip foil or plastic under furniture legs.

Stay out of contaminated water

Water from a sewer, toilet or outside flooding is unsafe for people and pets to wade through.

Dial 24/7 dispatch

Mention a nearby landmark, such as Grant Rea Park or Bicknell Park, plus what has been shut off.

Parks, Landmarks and Older Structures

Landmarks such as Grant Rea Park, Montebello City Park and the Montebello Municipal Golf Course help crews locate you quickly. The Juan Matias Sanchez Adobe, built about 1844 to 1845, reminds us that some properties carry historic value. If yours does, ask the city about review before changing anything original.

The Whittier Narrows area is nearby. Part of Pico Rivera's Montebello Gardens area shares the Montebello Unified School District.

What Montebello Properties Usually Need, in Order

Flooding from outside or a large pipe break starts with flood damage restoration. Most interior leaks lead to water mitigation first, then water damage repair once everything tests dry. If a damp cavity lingers, mold remediation follows the ANSI/IICRC S520 standard.

General Insurance Guidance

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not. Keep a claim diary, photograph the damage and make only temporary repairs until the adjuster visits. Ask your insurer to point to the exact policy provision if a limit or exclusion is cited.
