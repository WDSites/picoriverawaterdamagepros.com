This site has no contact form on purpose. A form waits until someone reads it, and a wet ceiling will not wait. Call the 24/7 dispatch line, describe what is happening, and the next step is arranged on that call.

For questions that are not urgent, such as a note about a past job or a general question, email contact@picoriverawaterdamagepros.com.

What to have ready when you call

You do not need to prepare much. A few details help the dispatcher move quickly, and you can give them from your phone while standing in the room.

  • The street address and the nearest cross streets
  • What happened and roughly when it started
  • Whether water is still flowing or pooling
  • Which rooms or floors are wet
  • Whether you could shut off the main water valve
  • Whether the power is still on in wet areas
  • Your insurance company's name, if you have it handy
  • Photos of the damage, taken before you move anything

Photographs and notes matter more than people expect. The California Department of Insurance advises homeowners to take pictures and keep records of cleanup and repair costs, so start your record now, even before the crew arrives.

If it is an emergency right now

Some situations need a different first call than ours.

Electrical or gas risk

If water is touching outlets, a panel or appliances, or you smell gas, get everyone out and call 911 or your utility before calling us.

Water you can stop

If a supply line is gushing and the main valve is reachable and dry, shut it off. Then call the 24/7 line.

Sewage or storm water

Keep people and pets out. Do not wade through it. Call the line and wait for guidance.

Everything else

Lift small valuables off wet floors, open nothing that is behind a wet ceiling, and call.

Describe the damage to a live dispatcher and get a clear next step.
