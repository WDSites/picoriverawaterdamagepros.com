Uptown Whittier Around Philadelphia Street and Greenleaf Avenue

Uptown is centered on Philadelphia Street and Greenleaf Avenue. If your building has one unit stacked over another business or home, a supply-line leak upstairs can drip through the shared ceiling below. Shut off the supply to your own space first. Then tell whoever sits underneath, because wet drywall sags fast and that second ceiling is often where the real damage shows up.

Original plaster, wood trim and old flooring are worth saving when they can be saved. Photograph each room before anyone touches it, tell the crew what is original, and ask for drying in place before any tear-out.

What the 1987 Whittier Narrows Earthquake Means for Older Buildings

The Whittier Narrows earthquake struck on October 1, 1987, at magnitude 5.9, and it damaged many historic Uptown buildings. The Whittier Conservancy formed that same year to stop demolition of historic buildings.

For a water loss, the practical lesson is restraint. If your property is older masonry or sits in a historic district, ask the city whether any review applies before repair work changes original features. Keep demolition limited to material that is truly wet, and keep the removed pieces if the city asks to see them.

College Hills and the Hillside Streets Above

College Hills, around Whittier College, is a historic district of 1920s and 1930s tracts, and Starlite Estates climbs into the hills nearby. On a sloped lot, water from a failed line can run downhill through a garage or lower room before you see it. Note where the water is exiting and which way it travels, then pass that to the dispatcher so the crew checks the low points first.

East Whittier: Friendly Hills, Murphy Ranch, Leffingwell Ranch and Sunglow

These neighborhoods run to the far east side of the city, the longest drive from our Pico Rivera base. Give the dispatcher your cross street and nearest landmark so the crew does not lose time finding you.

Whittier grew in stages after World War II, when orange groves were subdivided and a 1961 annexation added over 28,000 people. That means two houses on one block may hide different finishes behind the walls. Mention any remodel, since extra flooring layers hold water longer.

When water is spreading in Whittier

Moving water is the problem to solve first. Stop the source if you can, then call the 24/7 dispatch line. If water is reaching outlets, the panel or a gas appliance, stay back and call the utility or 911 before you call us.

Before We Arrive: If Water Is Still Spreading in Whittier

Close the valve

Shut the fixture valve under the sink or toilet. If the line is bursting, close the main valve for the house.

Cut power only if it is dry

Switch off breakers serving wet rooms only when the panel is dry and easy to reach. Otherwise stay away and phone the utility.

Raise what you can carry

Move rugs, books and electronics up onto dry counters. Prop up couch legs on foil or plastic.

Keep everyone out of dirty water

Kids and pets stay clear of anything from a toilet, drain or storm backup. Floodwater can carry contamination.

Call the 24/7 line

What Drives the Cost of a Whittier Water Loss

Five things set the price of any loss, and none of them can be judged from a phone call.

  • Water category 1, 2 or 3, which decides what must be removed
  • How much floor, wall and ceiling area is affected
  • Materials involved, such as plaster, hardwood or masonry
  • Drying days and the amount of equipment running
  • Rebuild scope once the structure is dry

The real number comes after someone sees the loss. Scope and price are explained before work starts.

A General Note on Insurance

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, while gradual leaks and neglect often are not. Flood is usually excluded and needs separate flood coverage. Take photos, keep receipts and read your policy, then ask your insurer what applies to your loss.

What to do first and what to ask

Whatever the age of the home, the first steps are the same: shut off the main water valve if you can reach it, turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and safe, lift valuables off wet floors, and photograph the damage before anything moves. Then call the 24/7 line.

When you compare companies, ask whether crews work to the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration and how drying will be verified with moisture readings.
