About Pico Rivera Water Damage Pros

A single 24/7 dispatch line for water damage and mold in Pico Rivera, California and the towns around it.

☎ Call (562) 271-5850

Pico Rivera Water Damage Pros is a water damage and mold response line for Pico Rivera, California. You call one number, describe what happened, and a licensed and insured restoration professional is sent to your address, with same-day response when a crew is available. The line is answered around the clock because water does not wait for business hours.

The business address is 8600-8800 Whittier Blvd in Pico Rivera, on the historic Whittier Boulevard corridor. The phone number is (562) 271-5850 and the email for non-urgent questions is contact@picoriverawaterdamagepros.com.

What we handle

The work falls into six services: water mitigation, emergency water removal, water damage cleanup, flood damage restoration, water damage repair and mold remediation. Water damage is the core of the business. Mold is the second service, because the two so often arrive together.

Equipment on a typical job includes moisture meters, thermal imaging cameras, extraction units, air movers and LGR dehumidifiers. Mold jobs add containment barriers, negative air machines and HEPA air scrubbers.

What we do not handle

Honesty about limits is part of being useful. This business does not do fire and smoke restoration, foundation repair, or waterproofing and exterior drainage work. If your problem is water entering from outside because of grading or a failing foundation, a drainage or foundation contractor is the right person for that part.

We also do not predict insurance outcomes. Whether a claim is paid is between you and your insurer, and we will not tell you otherwise.

How a call works

The process is deliberately plain, and each step has a purpose.

1. You call the 24/7 line

Give the address, the nearest cross streets and a short description. If water is still moving, shut off the main valve first if you can reach it safely.

2. Assessment on site

Moisture readings, photos and a look at what is wet and why. This is where the scope of work is built.

3. Scope and price explained

Before work starts, you hear what will be done, what it involves and how the price is set. No equipment goes in before that conversation.

4. Dry, clean, then rebuild

Extraction and drying come first, cleanup second and repairs last, with documentation you can hand to your insurer.

Standards we point homeowners to

Two published industry standards guide this kind of work. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers professional water damage restoration and ANSI/IICRC S520 covers professional mold remediation. They are worth knowing about because you can ask any company you are considering, including us, whether its crews work to them.

Beyond standards, ask any restoration company for proof of its California contractor license and its insurance before work starts. That advice applies to everyone, and it costs you one question.

What you will not find on this site

You will not find customer reviews, star ratings, job counts or awards here, because none are published without real records behind them. You will not find invented staff bios or stock-photo faces either. The site is built to give you accurate information and one reliable phone number.

Local detail on these pages comes from city, county and state sources, such as the City of Pico Rivera and the California Department of Insurance. Where we could not confirm a detail, the page tells you who to ask, such as the city Building Division or your water provider.

What to do before help arrives

Our advice on the phone is the same as it is here, and it is safe to start before anyone arrives.

  • Shut off the main water valve if you can reach it.
  • Switch off power to wet areas only when the panel is dry and safe to reach.
  • Do not walk through water that may be contaminated.
  • Take photos before anything is moved.
